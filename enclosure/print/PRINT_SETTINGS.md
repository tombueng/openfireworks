# OpenFireworks enclosure — print package

Generated by `make_print.py` from `../gen_enclosure.py` (never edit the STL/3MF by hand; change the generator and
re-run). Every part is already in its print orientation and laid out on its plate.

| plate | file | contents | extruder | time* | filament* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — **print first** | `plate0_fit_coupon.3mf` | fit coupon: a full-depth 28 mm strip of the west end of base + lid — both west snaps with their release buttons, the 12 west jack half-holes, the 12 west window pockets — plus 3 window plugs (2 channel, 1 status) | 1 case, 2 clear | ~3 h 50 | 30.5 g + 0.7 g |
| 1 | `plate1_lid_windows.3mf` | lid (top face down) + the 26 clear window plugs **beside it** (visible face down) | lid 1, windows 2 | ~5 h 40 | 36.6 g + 4.1 g |
| 2 | `plate2_base.3mf` | base (bottom face down, turned 90°), the 4 release buttons printed in place | 1 | ~6 h 50 | 64.5 g |

\* PrusaSlicer 2.9 with `prusaslicer_2head_petg.ini` (generic 2-head Marlin printer, 250 × 210 bed, incl. the
wipe tower at the back right). Your printer's own profile gives the real numbers. Everything stays ≥ 2 mm inside
a 250 × 210 bed, incl. skirt, brim and wipe tower; no prime lines at the bed edge.

**What the 3MF files carry** (PrusaSlicer project data; OrcaSlicer / Bambu Studio import it as a PrusaSlicer
project — check after import):
- object → extruder: lid / base / coupon strips → **1**, `windows` → **2** (clear);
- per object: windows 100 % rectilinear infill, monotonic top/bottom, 2 perimeters, elephant foot 0.1, seams at
  the corners (aligned); coupon strips 5 mm brim;
- **painted seams**: seam *blockers* on the tight fits (jack half-holes, the release buttons' guide cylinder, web
  hole and print-in-place gap faces) and seam *enforcers* on hidden faces next to them (inner face of the W/E
  walls, the buttons' flush push face);
- custom G-code at z 3.0: **nozzle 2 off** once the windows are done.

The STL files (`plateN_<object>.stl`) are the same objects already in plate coordinates (they do NOT carry the
painted seams or the extruder assignment). The 24 rectangular channel plugs (6.3 × 6.7) and the **2 square status
plugs (6.3 × 6.3, with a small dimple in one corner of their back)** — the status plugs go into the two windows in
the middle of the lid.

## Materials
- **Case (extruder 1): PETG** (ABS/ASA also fine). Any opaque colour; dark colours keep the LED light inside its
  own window.
- **Windows (extruder 2): clear / transparent PETG.** Printed they come out frosted, which is intended: they
  diffuse the LED.

## Settings — chosen for strength and repeatable fits
Use your printer's PETG profile and set/check these (all in `prusaslicer_2head_petg.ini`):

| setting | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| nozzle / layer | 0.4 mm / 0.20 mm (first layer 0.20) | the fits (0.1–0.4 mm gaps) are sized for this |
| perimeters | **4** | W/E walls (1.6 mm) 4 lines, tongues (1.2→0.8 mm) 2 lines, window webs (1.0 mm) 2 lines; N/S walls 4.8 mm |
| top / bottom layers | 5 / 5 | lid plate fully solid |
| infill | 40 % gyroid (windows 100 %, per object) | stiff base floor |
| temperature | 240 °C case, 245 °C clear; bed 80 °C; idle nozzle 170 °C | hot = good layer bonding at the upright tongue roots |
| fan | 20–40 % (clear 15–30 %), off for the first 3 layers, bridges 100 % | low fan for strong layers |
| **elephant-foot compensation** | **0.2 mm** (windows 0.1) | the lid's window pockets lie on the bed; it also opens the pockets' first layer by up to 0.2 mm per side, so expect a thin ring (≤ 0.4 mm) around each window on the top face |
| supports | **none** | designed support-free (45° roofs and keels, a 45° corbel under the board ledge, teardrop holes above the buttons). Short unsupported spots that print fine: the lid's R3 top rim on the bed (first ~0.9 mm), small flat roofs in the switch finger recess and the USB-C recess, the 0.3 mm window ledges, the 30° hook / catch faces and the bottoms of the button faces — the coupon shows how these come out |
| seam | **random** + the painted blockers/enforcers | measured by `check_print.py` (below) |
| XY / hole / shrinkage compensation | 0 | the clearances are in the model; see the pitch check below |
| ironing | off | |
| end G-code | both nozzles, bed and fan off | |

**Seam check** (`check_print.py`, 2026-11-22): per plate at most 2 seams start mid-face on a fit; the rest of the
seams near a fit sit at a loop corner (31 / 18 / 47 on plates 0 / 1 / 2) or at the crown / bottom end of a hole
(9 / 3 / 9), where the jack (soldered to the board, 0.1 mm play) and the button (teardrop roof above it) do not
bear. The window pockets are closed loops made only of fit faces, so one seam per layer lands on each pocket wall;
the plugs have 0.15 mm play per side and are glued, which absorbs that bump.

## Print order and checks
1. **Plate 0, fit coupon** — with exactly the settings you will use for the case.
   - *Buttons*: press each once — the two break-away ribs snap with a small click; the button then moves freely.
     Put the lid strip on the base strip: both tongues **click** in and the seam closes. It must not come off by
     pulling or tilting. Press both buttons: the lid strip comes free and lifts. Press one button 20 × hard to
     its stop (the tongue is printed upright, its layers carry the bending). Too loose → raise `HOOK_PRE`
     (0.05–0.1); a button sticks → larger gaps (`FACE_CLR`, `PIPX`, `PIPZ`, `PIP_Y` +0.05); a rib did not print → raise `RIB_L` / `RIB_W` slightly.
   - *Jacks*: lay a PJ-320A into a lower half-hole, put the lid strip on: the nose sits without forcing and
     without visible play. Too tight → raise `FIT_CLR` in `gen_enclosure.py` (0.10 → 0.15).
   - *Windows*: the channel plug goes in by hand from the outside, smooth (bed) face up, and sits flush on its
     ledge. Too tight → raise `WIN_PLUG_CLR`.
   Change a parameter → re-run `gen_enclosure.py`, `make_print.py`, `check_print.py`, `check_fit.py`.
2. **Plate 1, lid + windows** (multi-head). **Pitch check:** on the printed lid, measure from the first to the
   last jack half-hole on one side — it must be **88.0 mm** (± 0.1). PETG shrinks ~0.2–0.4 %, at the outer
   jacks about as much as the 0.1 mm nose play; if it is short, set the slicer's XY shrinkage compensation to the
   measured percentage and reprint (the base needs the same value).
3. **Plate 2, base.**

## After printing
- Press each release button once (ribs break), then press all four together: the lid lifts (about 0.5 mm; the
  full lift needs nearly the whole 1.2 mm button travel).
- Window plugs: insert from the outside, smooth bed face up. Fix each with a small drop of **clear UV resin or
  clear 2-part epoxy** on the spigot. **No superglue (CA)** — its vapour fogs clear PETG.
- Above every button the outer face shows a small pointed opening (the teardrop roof of the guide hole, about
  1.4 mm tall) — it is intended: a round hole would sag onto the printed-in-place button and weld it.
- **Use the lid as the jack soldering jig**: `docs/RESPIN3_BUILD_AND_FIRMWARE.md` §4 step 6.

tools/cadenv/bin/python enclosure/gen_enclosure.py
tools/cadenv/bin/python enclosure/print/make_print.py
